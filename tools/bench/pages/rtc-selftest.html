Summit Peer-to-Peer self test

Without a camera: a canvas is the video source and an oscillator the audio. ?camera=1 uses the camera and microphone, ?screen=1 asks to share a screen (both need a click on Allow). Sharing needs a user gesture: click Share screen when the title says so, or call rtcShareScreen() from the Developer Tools console (its evaluations count as gestures). The title ends as rtc selftest: N/M with the failures listed.
