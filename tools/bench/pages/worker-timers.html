JavaScriptCore timers across threads

Workers compile WebAssembly asynchronously over and over while the page keeps creating frames. An asynchronous compilation finishes on a compiler thread, which arms the worker's timer; the worker fires timers of its own; the page's main thread arms its garbage-collection timer for every new frame. All of them go through one JavaScriptCore lock. On Haiku, arming a timer used to wait for the looper of the timer's thread, which that thread holds while a timer fires, so the threads could deadlock and take the main thread with them: Proton Mail's sign-in page froze that way, and 1Password's popup waited on it forever. The result is posted after seconds (20); a deadlocked page posts nothing, which run-probe.py reports as a timeout.
