A call between two browsers through tools/bench/rtc-signal.py: open ?role=a on one machine and ?role=b on the other (same &room=). &camera=1 sends the camera and microphone, otherwise a canvas and a tone; &codec=H264 (or VP8, VP9) is preferred for the video this side receives, and &sendvideo=0 sends none. The title ends as rtc call: ... after &seconds= (default 12) of call.
